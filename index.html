EvoBalls Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

In short

EvoBalls does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. The game has no sign-up, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and makes no network requests of any kind.

What data is processed

The app stores only two things, and only on the device itself:

  • your best score and the highest number you have reached;
  • settings - sound, vibration and the chosen language.

This data lives in the local storage of the app. It is never sent anywhere and is not accessible to us or to any third party. It is removed together with the app, or by the Reset best score button in settings.

Permissions

The app requests no access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, files, calendar or any other device data. The only system feature it uses is vibration, and it can be turned off in settings.

Network

The app works fully offline. It needs no internet connection to start or to play.

Children

The game collects no data, so it is safe for any age and complies with COPPA and GDPR-K with regard to the collection of data from minors.

Third-party services

None are used: no ad networks, no analytics, no social media SDKs, no cloud saves.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions: vlad2008.kovalenko@gmail.com

Политика конфиденциальности EvoBalls

Дата вступления в силу: 18 августа 2026 года
